## Design Context

### Users
Founders, CTOs and product leads at startups and product teams who have an LLM or RAG problem and are deciding whether to hire an AI consultant. They arrive from a referral, a blog post or a search, and make a judgment within about 30 seconds. The job to be done: understand what Mubaraq does, trust that he has shipped real systems, and book a consultation. A secondary audience is readers of the blog (hosted on Hashnode as "The Digital Matrix").

### Brand Personality
Artistic, confident, memorable. The site is a piece of work in its own right: it shows taste and craft the moment it loads, and the visuals are part of the argument that this practitioner builds things with care. Bold over polite. It should be remembered, not merely approved of.

### Aesthetic Direction
Art catalogue meets generative studio. Monumental type (Syne ExtraBold against Instrument Serif italic), generative drawing, full-bleed colour changes between sections (dark, drafting paper, vivid blue), film grain, expressive motion and interaction. No restraint requirement: use large scale, strong contrast, layered effects and surprising details wherever they make the site more distinctive. The blue accent `#3F8CFF` on tinted near-black `#0D0F18` is the core palette; paper `#ECE8DF` and full-bleed blue are used as set pieces.

Avoid looking templated: generic "AI startup" patterns (stock gradient orbs, identical icon-card grids, default glassmorphism). Original, hand-made-feeling versions of bold effects are welcome.

### Design Principles
1. **Be memorable.** Every section should have something worth looking at twice.
2. **One clear action.** "Book a Consultation" stays unmistakable at every scroll depth, however loud everything else gets.
3. **Type and drawing carry the design.** Scale, contrast and generative art do the work.
4. **Motion is part of the material.** Scroll, pointer and load animation are encouraged, as long as the page stays fast.
5. **Accessible by default.** Meet WCAG AA contrast, keep visible focus states and keyboard access, and honour `prefers-reduced-motion` by showing still versions of the art.
